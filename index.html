How to Play Simon

1

Watch & Listen

Simon will flash a button and play its distinct audio tone. Memorize the color and order.

2

Repeat the Sequence

Tap, click, or use keyboard keys to repeat the exact sequence Simon just performed.

3

Level Up & Expand

Every round you get right, Simon adds one more step to the pattern. How long can your memory last?

4

Avoid Mistakes

One incorrect tap triggers Game Over! Check your high score and challenge yourself to beat it.

Controls Guide

  • Mobile & Tablet: Tap the colored buttons directly on screen.
  • Desktop: Mouse click or keyboard shortcuts:
    Q Green W Red A Yellow S Blue Space Start / Restart
